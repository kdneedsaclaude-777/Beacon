# Beacon Terms of Service

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

By using Beacon, you agree to these terms. They exist for one reason: to keep Beacon useful, kind, and safe for the whole neighbourhood.

## 1. What Beacon is (and isn't)
Beacon is a community awareness tool. Reports are submitted by community members and are **not verified by police or by Beacon**. Beacon is **not an emergency service** — in an emergency, always call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the US) first.

## 2. Who can use Beacon
Anyone can browse the map. **Posting reports requires an age group of 13 or older.** Under-13 profiles are view-only.

## 3. Community rules
When posting, you agree to:
- **Report actions and events, not people's appearance.** Never describe race, ethnicity, or religion. Reports that do so will be removed.
- **Never post exact addresses or unit numbers.** Block-level map pins are enough.
- **Never accuse a named or identifiable person.** Beacon is not a courtroom.
- **Photos of property and scenes only — never of people.**
- **Post honestly.** No jokes, spam, exaggerations, or false reports.
- **Use respectful language.** Profanity and slurs are automatically blocked.

## 4. Moderation and enforcement
- Posts with inappropriate language are blocked automatically. **Three violations permanently remove posting access** on your device.
- Any user can flag a post; flagged posts are hidden pending review, and posts flagged by multiple community members are hidden for everyone.
- Reports expire from the default map view after 30 days.
- Beacon may remove any content or restrict any user to protect the community.

## 5. Your content
You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you give Beacon permission to store your report and display it to other users. You can delete your own reports at any time.

## 6. No warranty
Beacon is provided "as is." Report information may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Do not make safety-critical decisions based solely on Beacon. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Beacon and its creator are not liable for damages arising from use of the app or reliance on its content.

## 7. Do not misuse Beacon
Do not use Beacon to harass, stalk, profile, or organize action against any person or group; to interfere with the service; or for any unlawful purpose. Vigilantism is contrary to everything Beacon stands for: **awareness, not confrontation.**

## 8. Changes
These terms may be updated; continued use after an update means you accept the new terms. Material changes will be announced in the app.
