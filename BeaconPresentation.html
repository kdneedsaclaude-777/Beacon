Beacon

See your neighbourhood clearly.

A free community safety app, built for our neighbourhood — by a neighbour.

Created by KDragonkdneedsaclaude@gmail.com

The problem

We find out about things too late

  • A car gets broken into — the street hears about it days later, if at all
  • A scam caller works through the phone book — nobody warns the next person
  • Word travels through group chats some neighbours aren't in — especially seniors

The idea

One shared map, by neighbours, for neighbours

  • See recent incidents near you — thefts, break-ins, scams, suspicious activity
  • Post what you saw in under a minute — anonymously
  • Get the picture for your own street, not the whole city

Awareness, not alarm. Beacon informs — it never replaces 911.

How it works

Three things, all simple

🗺
1

Look

A map of your area. Prefer words to maps? Switch to a big-print list.

✍️
2

Report

Six easy questions, one at a time. There's even a practice mode to learn on.

👁
3

Confirm

Saw the same thing? Tap "I saw this too" — no duplicate posts needed.

Built kind, on purpose

Safety apps can go wrong.
Beacon is designed not to.

🤝

Actions, not appearance

Reports describe what happened — never race, ethnicity, or how someone looks.

🔒

Privacy protected

No exact addresses allowed. Locations round to the block. Reports are anonymous.

🧹

Real moderation

Bad language is blocked automatically. Three strikes removes posting. Neighbours can flag anything.

🚫

No comment wars

No comment sections — that's where neighbourhood apps turn nasty. And your data is never sold. Ever.

For the whole family

Grandkids to grandparents

  • Age-fitted experience — we ask an age group, never a birthday
  • Family filter — harsh words are blurred if little eyes glance at the screen
  • Under-13 explorer mode — kids can look and learn, but can't post
  • Age-matched safety tips — walking-to-school tips for kids, scam defence for seniors

Our promise

No ads.
No trackers.
No accounts or emails.
Your location is never shared.
Your data is never sold — ever.

Beacon has no server of its own — reports live in Apple's encrypted iCloud, and your profile never leaves your phone.
