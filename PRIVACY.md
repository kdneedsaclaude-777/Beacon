# Beacon Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

Beacon is a community safety app built on one promise: **your data is not the product.** No ads, no trackers, no analytics companies, and nothing is ever sold or shared with third parties. Ever.

## What Beacon stores, and where

### On your device only (never uploaded)
- **Your profile** — nickname, avatar, and age group. Beacon never asks for your real name, email, phone number, or birthday.
- **Your settings** — theme, text size, trusted contact, reminder times.
- **Your votes** — which reports you confirmed or thumbs-downed.
- **Your location** — used live to centre the map and measure distances. Beacon never stores your location history and never shares your position with other users or anyone else.

### Shared with your community (only when you choose to post)
When you post a report, that report — category, date and time, an **approximate block-level location** (never an exact address), your description, and any photo you attach — becomes visible to other Beacon users nearby. Reports are anonymous: they carry no name, profile, or device information.

When community sync is enabled, reports are stored in Apple's **iCloud (CloudKit)** service, encrypted in transit and at rest, in a public database readable by other Beacon users. Beacon has no server of its own and no employee-accessible database.

## Location permission
Beacon asks for **"while using the app"** location access, used to: centre the map on your area, show distances to reports, filter "In My Area," and (optionally) attach a block-rounded location to a report you post. You can use most of Beacon without granting location access.

## Photos
Photo attachments are optional, chosen by you, and community guidelines require they show **property and scenes only — never people**. Photos you attach are shared as part of your public report.

## Children
Beacon collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Profiles for users under 13 are view-only ("Explorer mode") and cannot post. The family filter hides harsh language for minors and cannot be disabled on minors' profiles.

## Deleting your data
- Delete any report you posted from the report's own screen — this also removes it from the shared database.
- Delete the app to remove all local data (profile, settings, votes).

## What Beacon never does
- No advertising or ad networks
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
- No selling, renting, or sharing of data
- No accounts, passwords, or email collection
- No background location tracking

## Emergencies
Beacon is not an emergency service. Reports are community-submitted and not verified by police. In an emergency, always call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the US).
